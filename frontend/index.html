What is an ATS?

Understanding the gatekeeper between you and your dream job

🤖

Applicant Tracking System

An ATS is software used by 99% of Fortune 500 companies to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. It scans for keywords, formatting, and relevant experience.

📊

The Hard Truth

75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before reaching a recruiter. Even qualified candidates get filtered out due to poor formatting or missing keywords.

🎯

Why It Matters

Your resume might be perfect for the job, but if it's not optimized for ATS, it won't matter. The right keywords and structure can make all the difference.
